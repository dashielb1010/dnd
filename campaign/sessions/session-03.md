# Session 3 — The Boonta Feast

## Synopsis
Four separate threads converged on a stretch of coast west of Velen, drawn together by the smell of smoked wild boar. A feral halfling pitmaster, two shipwrecked sailors, a displaced dragonborn monk, and a cheerful elf with a sentient log shared a meal on the beach and decided to travel together to Velen.

## Intro Threads

### Shamsii & Moru (Brad, Casey-B)
Their Tethyrian merchant ship — bound for Velen with block salt and dry goods — was attacked by three pirate ships in a coordinated raid. The captain, first mate, and crew were killed. Shamsii and Moru jumped overboard and swam 10–15 miles back to the coast, washing up exhausted on the northern shore of the Dragon's Head.

### Kai's Character & Cornbread (Kai, Casey-M)
Kai's character was mystically displaced from his monastery in southern Durmista to the Dragon's Head peninsula during a deep meditative trance (see session 3.1). He met Cornbread in the forest — a petite elf who treats forest teleportation as perfectly normal — and the two wandered together toward the coast.

### Lester (James)
A halfling from an island northwest of Velen. Snuck aboard a visiting ship to scavenge ingredients, fell asleep, woke up at sea. Hid until thirst forced him out, was discovered, decided he'd rather be on his own, and jumped overboard. Swam to shore and immediately did what he does best: set a trap for Boonta.

## What Happened

1. Lester, having reached the coast, trapped and killed a wild boar and dug a pit to smoke it — the art he's perfected over a lifetime on his island.
2. **Shamsii and Moru**, travelling east along the coast after washing ashore, caught the scent of smoked Boonta drifting through the air.
3. **Kai's character and Cornbread**, emerging from the forest toward the coast, caught it too.
4. Both pairs followed the aroma to **Lester**, who — generous as ever — invited them all to share.
5. The five strangers sat down together on the beach and ate. Moru, impressed, insisted Lester show her how he spiced the meat.
6. Over the meal, introductions were made. An odd band: a proud Calimite cat-man, a terrifying half-orc monk, a sanguine dragonborn flautist, a fey-touched elf with a log for a pet, and a feral halfling pitmaster who could barely explain how he'd gotten there.
7. They decided, easily enough, that they might stick together for a while.
8. **Shamsii**, drawing on his years as a navigator, reckoned Velen was about 10 miles east of their position. He also knew they ought to inform the Dockmaster that the merchant shipment had been lost to pirates.
9. The party set off east toward Velen.

## Key Decisions
- Everyone chose to share a meal with strangers — the simplest and most human (and halfling, and elven, and dragonborn) of bonds
- The group decided to stick together rather than go their separate ways
- Shamsii took the lead on navigation and set the immediate objective: report the pirate attack to the Velen Dockmaster
