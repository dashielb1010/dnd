# Session 2 — The Ingrevassa Shore

## Synopsis
A solo introductory session for Galen's character, Shanu Danu-Vavich. A Wood Elf druid and his war band intercepted pirate slavers on the sacred shores of the Wealdath, pursued and captured their ship, and freed over twenty captives. Shanu then sailed the captured vessel west toward Velen — his first step out of the forest and into the wider world.

## What Happened

1. Dusk on the northern shore of the Dragon's Neck. Shanu and a party of young Ingrevassa warriors were on patrol, a day's journey from their homesteads, when they spotted intruders on the beach.
2. Two rowboats had been beached above the tide line. Five or six goblins squatted near a creek, filling barrels and skins with fresh water. An orc in a leather coat with nautical insignias stood guard, a rusted cutlass at his belt. A ship waited somewhere beyond the headland.
3. Sacred land. Intruders. The elves ambushed from the treeline and fought the landing party — over two dozen enemies in total. The Ingrevassa prevailed, though two of their brethren fell and many were wounded.
4. The elves monitored the orcish slaver barge as it set sail west, hugging the coastline. Realizing the ship would soon reach a cluster of neighboring tribes' fishing villages, they ran cross-country to warn their neighbors and muster defenses.
5. At a rocky cape near the villages, the combined force sprung a second ambush. Archers fired waves of arrows from the cliffs while warriors boarded the ship from fishing boats. Every last greenskinned slaver was killed.
6. They freed more than twenty captives — people from far-flung origins and nearby villages alike.
7. After resting, Shanu decided to sail the captured orcish vessel west to the town of men called Velen. A skeleton crew of freed captives helped man the ship — no easy task, given its crude and brutish construction, built by and for creatures with more stamina than wit.
8. The journey to Velen would take a tenday.

## Key Decisions
- The Ingrevassa chose to pursue the ship rather than let it pass — protecting neighboring tribes at cost to themselves
- Shanu chose to sail to Velen rather than return home — stepping outside the forest for the first time
