# Session 1 — The Calim Desert

## Synopsis
What was planned as a one-shot. Three strangers — a proud Tabaxi half-elf, a terrifying half-orc monk, and a Calimite cleric — escaped a slave caravan in the Calim Desert, stumbled into a Djinn's trial, and fought their way through to rescue a pregnant woman from a Calif's court. One of the best session openers the table has had.

## What Happened

1. The party — Shamsii, Moru, and Hasseid — found themselves chained in a slave cart, captured independently, being transported across the Calim Desert by a local Calif's caravan. The slaves were being moved from the Calif's main estate to a holiday residence.
2. Unknown to the party, a young woman from the Calif's harem had been plotting her escape. She was pregnant, and as a concubine rather than a proper wife, would not be allowed to keep the child. She'd enlisted one of the Calif's Tabaxi harem guards — who had secretly fallen in love with her — to help her flee.
3. The escape attempt kicked off mid-transport. Something like a magical grenade landed in the slave cart, exploding and sending it tumbling down a dune. Chaos erupted across the caravan.
4. The players fought off slavers while struggling to free themselves, capitalizing on the confusion. Once they'd dealt with their immediate captors, they spotted a young woman galloping away from the caravan with robes streaming behind her, a mounted Tabaxi riding alongside, and a dozen horsemen in pursuit.
5. The players followed the tracks across the desert and found a cluster of knuckle-shaped plateaus with oases at their bases — and a suspicious number of horses parked nearby.
6. Inside a cave, they found a large chamber. The Tabaxi guard was slumped against a stone, barely alive and unconscious. An ornate urn sat before an ominous circular carving on the far wall.
7. A misty figure emerged from the urn — a Djinn. It offered a deal: pass three challenges, and it would free them.
8. **Challenge 1**: A fight with a bone naga.
9. **Challenge 2**: A fight with a pair of shades guarding a portal gateway the Djinn opened.
10. **Challenge 3**: "Wait, what's the third thing?" asked a player, as the portal cleared to reveal the throne room of the Calif's estate — the young woman about to be executed for her insolence. *"Save the child,"* said the Djinn.
11. A fight ensued in the throne room to rescue the young woman. The session ended mid-escape, with the party having intervened but not yet clear of the estate.

## Key Decisions
- The party chose to follow the fleeing woman's tracks rather than simply escaping into the desert
- They engaged with the Djinn's bargain rather than trying to leave the cave
- They went through the portal into the Calif's stronghold — no hesitation
