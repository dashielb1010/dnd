# Lester

## Backstory Summary
A halfling from an island somewhere northwest of Velen. His life until very recently had been extraordinarily simple. A solitary creature, nearly animal in his ways, but kind in temperament. He knows almost nothing of his family, his history, or the wider world — and until recently, it barely crossed his mind to wonder.

Lester is an expert hunter and trapper of what he calls **"the Boonta"** — wild boars that inhabit the islands and coasts of the Sea of Swords, descendants of pigs brought ashore by sailors ages ago. He is a master at dispatching them and an artist at smoking them: he digs deep pits in the sand and slow-smokes the carcasses, enhancing the meat with herbs and fruits gathered from his island. His living was made in hunting, smoking, and sharing his bounty with the nearby families that shared the island with him. Little else.

Since when, exactly, has he been there? He's not sure. In fact it barely occurred to him to ask.

One day a ship came to the island. Lester — always on the take for new ingredients for his pits, and lacking the social instincts to simply ask — decided to board in the night and scavenge. Foraging, scavenging, stealing: all much the same to him. But he made a mistake. He fell asleep aboard. He woke the next morning on a ship that had sailed.

He hid for a time, but eventually had to come out for water. Discovered mid-drink, he sized up the situation — decided he'd be better off on his own — spotted the coast (far, but not too far), made a run for it, and jumped overboard. He swam to shore, wherever it happened to be, and once there did what he does best: he set a trap for Boonta.

## Goals & Motivations
- Hunt the Boonta. Smoke the Boonta. Share the Boonta.
- Got on a ship by accident — now he's in the world whether he likes it or not

## Key Relationships
- **The island families** — neighbors he shared his bounty with; not much deeper than that
- No known family of his own

## DM Notes
- The amnesia / lack of self-knowledge is a major hook — how long has he been on that island? Is he older than he thinks? Is he something other than a simple halfling?
- Island northwest of Velen — likely in or near the Nelanther Isles, which is pirate-infested territory. How has a gentle halfling pitmaster survived there?
- "The Boonta" is a great bit of character flavor — feral pigs as a regional feature of the Sea of Swords islands
- Wild, almost animal in his ways but kind — the anti-civilized character, pure instinct and generosity
- Left the island entirely by accident — snuck aboard a ship to scavenge ingredients, fell asleep, woke up at sea. The adventure chose him.
- Where did that ship end up? Presumably near Velen, converging with the other threads.
