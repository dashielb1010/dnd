# Shanu Danu-Vavich

## Backstory Summary
A young Wood Elf druid of the Ingrevassa Tribe, born in the deep woods of a peninsula of the Wealdath that juts into the Sea of Swords. The Ingrevassa live close to the roots of the world, and Shanu more than most. Well-regarded among his kin — not yet fully inducted into the Circle, but watched closely by its elders. In his season of growth, he patrols the sacred boundaries, tends grove and glade, and watches for the signs of monsters or men.

During session 2, Shanu led a patrol that intercepted pirate slavers on the northern shore of the Dragon's Neck, then pursued and captured their ship. He sailed the captured vessel west to Velen with a skeleton crew of freed captives, arriving after a tenday's difficult sailing.

## Goals & Motivations
- Protector of the Wealdath and his tribe's sacred lands
- Walking the path of balance — not yet a full member of the Circle

## Key Relationships
- **The Ingrevassa Tribe** — his people, deep in the Wealdath on the Dragon's Neck
- **The Circle elders** — watching his growth, yet to fully induct him
