# Cornbread

## Backstory Summary
A petite elvish woman with a fey-adjacent air about her. Travels with a tiny bat companion and a sentient log that follows her around like a dog. Has apparently been displaced by the forest before — multiple times — and treats it as a perfectly normal, even enjoyable, occurrence. "Oh yeah, that'll happen! I like it. I like to wander to new places."

Met Kai's character in the Wealdath after his mysterious displacement. They decided quite naturally to wander together and headed toward the ocean.

## Goals & Motivations
- Likes to wander. Likes new places. That seems to be about it — and that's enough.

## Key Relationships
- **Kai's character** — met in the forest; traveling companions by mutual, easy agreement
- **Tiny bat companion** — unnamed?
- **The sentient log** — follows her like a dog; nature and origin unknown

## DM Notes
- Cornbread's comfort with forest displacement suggests she's either fey-touched, has a deep connection to the Wealdath, or is something other than she appears
- The sentient log is delightful and begs explanation — awakened wood? Fey creature? A cursed something?
- Her casualness about teleporting forests is a potential worldbuilding thread — is this a known phenomenon in the deep Wealdath?
- Subclass/class unknown — the bat and log suggest druid, warlock, or something fey-pact-adjacent
