# Kai's Character (name TBD)

## Backstory Summary
A Gold Dragonborn Monk raised in a monastery along the southern edge of the Duchy of Durmista. Lived a contemplative life in harmony with his surroundings — training, meditating, and participating in the pastoral life of nearby farming communities that catered to travelers on the road between Durmista and the settled southern lands of Tethyr.

One evening after his daily training, he ventured out alone, lit a bonfire at the forest's edge with his breath, and sat playing his flute. The music carried him deeper and deeper into a meditative trance — and then the world changed around him. The fire faded, the trees shifted to unfamiliar ones, the air smelled of brine and wild growth. He had been transported, somehow, to an entirely different part of the forest.

He flew above the canopy (being a winged Dragonborn) and saw an unfamiliar landscape stretching in every direction, with ocean to the north. Ever sanguine, he simply began walking toward the sea — and soon encountered Cornbread.

## Goals & Motivations
- Deeply introspective and curious — leaned *into* the mysterious displacement rather than panicking
- Sanguine temperament; takes the world as it comes

## Key Relationships
- **Cornbread** (Casey-M's PC) — met in the forest after the displacement; decided naturally to wander together
- **The monastery** — his former home on the southern edge of Durmista; presumably wondering where he went
