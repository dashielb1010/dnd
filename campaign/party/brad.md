# Shamsii Kafa Niyaz al-Milh

## Backstory Summary
A Calimite Tabaxi/Half-Elf — nimble, outspoken, and proud. A formidable warrior and sailor. Was captured independently and enslaved, carted across the Calim Desert by a local Calif's caravan. Escaped during the chaos of session 1, completed a Djinn's three trials, and helped rescue a young woman from the Calif's court.

After the escape, Shamsii and Moru moved quietly and carefully until they reached Calimport, where they parted ways with the young mother and her Tabaxi lover. Shamsii — whose parents were sailors — got them a job on a trading vessel, and the two sailed together for roughly three years across multiple postings.

Their most recent posting was aboard a Tethyrian merchant ship sailing from one of the Kingdom's larger ports to Velen, carrying a shipment of block salt and dry goods. But pirate activity has spiked in recent years, and this time luck ran out. Three pirate ships attacked simultaneously. The captain, first mate, and the rest of the crew were killed as far as the two could tell. Shamsii and Moru jumped ship together, swimming 10–15 miles back to the coast, washing up on shore exhausted.
