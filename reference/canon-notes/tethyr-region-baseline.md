# Tethyr Region — Canon Baseline (as of 1492 DR)

What we established from canon, what's ambiguous, and where homebrew fills the gaps.

## Hard Canon (established in published sources)

- The Dragon's Neck is the eastern half of the Tethyr Peninsula; the Dragon's Head is the west. Divided by **Khalid's Wall** (built c. -378 DR). `[LOI, FRW]`
- The **Wealdath** (Forest of Tethir) is the largest forest in the Lands of Intrigue, covering most of the peninsula. Named for the elven hero Tethir (-11,700 DR). `[LOI]`
- Two elven tribes dominate the Wealdath: **Suldusk** (wild/green elves, SE) and **Elmanesse** (moon elves, west). They unified at some point pre-1370 DR. `[LOI]`
- **Suldanessellar** — elven treetop city built on the Tree of Life. Ruled by Queen Ellesime. Attacked by Jon Irenicus c. 1369 DR; liberated, Tree restored. Still exists. `[BG2, FRW]`
- **Myth Rhynn** — ancient elven tomb city with a decayed mythal. Raises non-elven dead as undead; sickens elves who enter. Possibly home to the lich Mallin. `[LOI, FRW]`
- **Duchy of Cape Velen** governs the peninsula. Three counties: Firedrake, Fyraven, Greenshores. Duke Llachior Blackthorn ruled c. 1370 DR. `[LOI]`
- **City of Velen** — haunted port city at the tip of the Dragon's Head. Castle Tordraken. Naval defense against Nelanther pirates. `[LOI]`
- **Mosstone** — walled caravan town, Trade Way, pop. up to 6,000. Founded 687 DR. `[LOI]`
- **Tethyrian Interregnum** (1347–1369 DR) — two decades of civil war after Castle Tethyr burned. `[LOI, FRW]`
- **The Reclamation** (1367–1369 DR) — Zaranda Star and Haedrak III restored the throne. Storm Siege of Myratma. `[LOI, FRW]`
- **1385 DR** — Spellplague. Widespread devastation. `[FRCG]`
- **1424 DR** — Cape Velen declared independence from Tethyr due to isolation caused by Muranndin. `[FRCG, FRW]`
- **Shieldmeet** occurs in 1492 DR. A day of open council, treaties, truces, druidic gatherings. `[FRW]`

## Canon Gaps (5e is silent — DM freedom)

- **Who rules Tethyr in 1492 DR?** Zaranda would be ~164. No 5e source confirms or denies her survival.
- **Cape Velen's current status.** Still independent? Reabsorbed? No update since 1424 DR.
- **Muranndin.** Still a monster-haunted wasteland, or reclaimed? No 5e source addresses it.
- **Wealdath elven tribes.** No post-Spellplague update. Likely still present in some form.
- **Suldanessellar's current state.** Confirmed to exist, no details on leadership or condition.
- **Shieldmeet 1492 DR.** No specific events described — blank canvas.

## Homebrew Decisions

### Who rules Tethyr? — Queen Chandaara Rhindaun
- Young queen, early twenties, crowned c. 1489–1490 DR
- Descendant of Zaranda Star and Haedrak III
- Fourth in line; three older siblings died in **separate tragic incidents**
- Parents (previous King and Queen) presided over post-Sundering recovery; both have passed
- Kingdom is anxious — young ruler, thinning bloodline, much to prove

### Muranndin — Remains largely untamed
- Not reclaimed by human civilization
- The Wealdath elves are the true barrier, holding back monstrous forces
- Elves live in balance with the wildness, embracing it as a natural boundary against "civilized" nations
- Tethyr's strength has held but not expanded

### Cape Velen — Brief independence, democratic reintegration
- Independence (1424 DR) lasted only ~10–20 years; the city-state couldn't sustain itself
- Facing harsh economic and environmental pressures, the people **voted** to rejoin Tethyr
- Condition: democratic representation in city and county governance
- Reintegrated c. 1440s DR; now nominally part of Tethyr but with semi-autonomous governance
- An aging Duke holds limited authority due to popular resistance to direct Tethyrian rule
- The city has grown markedly in recent decades
- Politics of Velen have been explored in play sessions — to be fleshed out further

### Calimshan — A new Caliphate rising
- A new Caliphate emerged and fought back against the Djinni occupation
- Reclaimed parts of Calimshan with marked success
- Pressure on Tethyr's southern border has moderated as a result

### Suldanessellar — Thriving but isolated
- The elven treetop city endures, prospering in the post-Sundering era
- Deliberately isolated from human affairs — not hostile, just removed
- Well outside the current campaign locality but strong potential as a future setting
- Queen Ellesime presumably still ruling (elven lifespan makes this trivial)

### The Wealdath Elves — Thriving
- Elves have thrived during the post-Spellplague era
- Vigilantly holding back evil and monstrous forces from Muranndin
- Living in balance with the wildness — embracing forests and mountains as natural boundaries
- Philosophy: the wild is not something to be tamed but a force to be respected
