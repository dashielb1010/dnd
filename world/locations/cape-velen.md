# Cape Velen

## Overview
Port city at the tip of the Dragon's Head Peninsula, capital of the Duchy of Cape Velen. Sits in a shallow bay between two ocean-side hills: **Captain's Cliff** (west) and **Widow's Hill** (east), with noble and wealthy homes covering both. Famous for being **exceptionally haunted** — though the citizens hardly notice.

As the westernmost major coastal city on mainland Faerun, Velen has been a stalwart defender against the **pirates of the Nelanther Isles** for centuries, maintaining a well-trained city guard and formidable naval forces.

The city has **grown markedly** in recent decades, becoming a significant urban center on the southern Sword Coast.

## Key Features
- **Castle Tordraken** — the ducal keep, atop a tall sea cliff on the northern seaside. Views of Acoval's Cove, the city, the Stoneblade, and the Downdragon Rise forests.
- **Captain's Cliff** and **Widow's Hill** — the twin hills flanking the harbor
- Haunted streets and buildings (the citizens are unfazed)

## Political Situation — The Reintegration
Velen's politics are unique in Tethyr and a source of ongoing tension:

- **1424 DR** — Cape Velen declared independence from Tethyr after Muranndin cut off overland routes.
- **c. 1430s DR** — Independence proved unsustainable. Harsh economic and environmental pressures mounted.
- **c. 1440s DR** — The people of Velen voted to rejoin the Kingdom of Tethyr, **on the condition** of having a say in the governance of the city and surrounding counties. A charter of democratic representation was established — the terms of reintegration.
- **1492 DR** — An **aging Duke** holds nominal authority, but his influence is limited by the people's resistance to direct rule from Tethyr. The city is **nominally reintegrated** but fiercely protective of its democratic concessions.
