# The Wealdath (Forest of Tethir)

## Overview
The largest forest in the Lands of Intrigue and one of the largest on the Sword Coast. Called **Wealdath** ("unspoiled woods") by the elves, it blankets the Dragon's Neck and much of the surrounding peninsula. Dense old-growth, full of small lakes, pools, streams, and cascades. Dark, cool, and wet — heavy rainfall, especially on the peninsula.

Named after the elven hero **Tethir**, the first elven dragonslayer, who in -11,700 DR sacrificed his life to kill two red dragons, buying his tribe time to escape into the southern reaches of the ancient forest of Keltormir.

## Inhabitants

### Elven Tribes
| Tribe | Subrace | Territory | Notes |
|-------|---------|-----------|-------|
| **Suldusk** | Wild elves (green) | Southeastern reaches (Durmista) | Only surviving original tribe of the forest |
| **Elmanesse** | Moon elves | Western Wealdath (Noromath) | Refugees from the fall of Myth Drannor |
| **Tethir** | — | — | Extinct by 1370 DR; original warrior tribe of Keltormir |

The Suldusk and Elmanesse eventually unified, creating a cohesive elven presence.

### Other Inhabitants
- **Fey**: Brownies, centaurs, dryads, satyrs, sprites, atomies, leprechauns
- **Monstrous races**: Bugbears, gnolls, hobgoblins, kobolds, norkers, ogres, orcs, trolls
- **Hags**: Green hags
- **Lycanthropes**: Present in notable numbers
- **Treants**: Forest guardians
- **Lythari**: Shapechanging elves; rumored portals to their realm hidden in the forest
- **Bandits**: Several bases within the forest
- **Dragons**: Linussaxannol (green), Buchembrallor (green), Garlokantha (gold), Arellaxerrontoal (bronze)

The western half has notably high populations of goblins, hobgoblins, centaurs, and satyrs.

## Nominal Human Governance
| Duchy | Elvish Meaning | Territory | Leader (c. 1370 DR) |
|-------|---------------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Noromath** | "Legacy of the North" | Western half, east of Trade Way | Duke Allain "Foxfire" Kevanarial (Elmanesse representative) |
| **Durmista** | "Forest of Twilight" | Southeastern reaches | Lord Rhothomir, Treespeaker of the Wealdath |

Both are "duchies in name only" — elven-controlled territories that humor human political labels.

## Key Landmarks

### Suldanessellar
The great elven treetop city, built atop the branches of the **Tree of Life**. A meeting place for all elves of the forest. Ruled by **Queen Ellesime**, whose eternal youth is sustained by the Tree. Famously featured in Baldur's Gate II, where the exiled mage Jon Irenicus broke through its defenses and poisoned the Tree of Life.

### Myth Rhynn
An ancient elven tomb city deep in the Mytharan Woods section. Built during the height of Keltormir — its age uncertain even to the elves. Protected by a **decayed mythal** that prevents elven dead from being raised as undead, but paradoxically raises non-elven creatures that die there. Any elf entering the area becomes nauseated. Home to will-o'-wisps and possibly the **lich Mallin**.

### Talltrees
A treetop village of the Elmanesse tribe. **Destroyed in 1364 DR** by the cutthroat captain Bunlap. Duke "Foxfire" occasionally returned to its ruined branches for meetings.

### Mosstone
Walled caravan-stop town on the Trade Way near the forest's edge. Founded 687 DR by King Strohm II (the Stag King), who was also its first Archdruid. Population up to 6,000 during busy caravan seasons. Cool, damp, shady — buildings have fresh springs in their cellars.
