# Kingdom of Tethyr

## Overview
A feudal monarchy in the Lands of Intrigue. Borders Amn to the north, Calimshan to the south, the Sea of Swords to the west, and the Shining Sea to the southeast. Organized as monarch > dukes > counts > sheriffs.

Tethyr's strength has held through generations of crisis — the Spellplague, the genie occupation of Calimshan, the isolation of its western peninsula — but it has not expanded. The kingdom is stable but not ascendant, and now carries the anxiety of a young, untested queen and a bloodline that has thinned alarmingly.

## Current Ruler — Queen Chandaara Rhindaun
- Descendant of Zaranda Star and Haedrak III
- Early twenties, crowned within the past 3 years (c. 1489–1490 DR)
- Fourth in line — her three older siblings all died tragically in **separate incidents**
- Her father (previous King) and mother presided over the post-Sundering recovery; both have passed
- The kingdom is anxious: a young queen, a thinning bloodline, and powerful neighbors
- See: [Queen Chandaara](../npcs/queen-chandaara.md)

## Neighboring Powers

### Calimshan (South)
A new Caliphate has emerged and fought back against the genies, reclaiming parts of Calimshan from the Djinni occupation (1385–1480s DR). Their marked success has moderated the pressure on Tethyr's southern border. The relationship is evolving — no longer an existential threat, but not yet a stable partner.

### Amn (North)
Functional but diminished mercantile state. Weakened by the Spellplague, wary of magic. Historical tensions with Tethyr over borders and trade persist but are muted.

### Muranndin (East of Peninsula)
Remains **largely untamed**. The monster-haunted wilderness between Amn and the Tethyr Peninsula has not been reclaimed by human civilization. The Wealdath elves have been the true barrier — vigilantly holding back monstrous forces while letting the forests and mountains flourish as a natural boundary against the "civilized" nations.

## Timeline

### The Founding Era
- **1347 DR** — King Alemander IV, Queen Rhinda, and four children killed when Castle Tethyr was set ablaze. Begins **two decades of civil war** (the Tethyrian Interregnum).
- **1367–1369 DR** — **The Reclamation.** Zaranda Star and Haedrak Rhindaun III restore the throne.
- **1369 DR** — **Storm Siege of Myratma.** Haedrak and Zaranda slay the usurper Prince Yusuf Jhannivvar.
- **Kythorn 30, 1369 DR** — Royal wedding of Queen Zaranda Star and King-Consort Haedrak III.

### The Spellplague Era (~1385–1440s DR)
- **1385 DR** — The Spellplague. Mystra murdered, the Weave unravels. Magic goes haywire.
- **1385 DR** — Calimshan falls to genie reconquest. Tethyr's southern border becomes a frontline. Refugees pour north.
- **1385+ DR** — Muranndin forms, cutting off overland routes to the Tethyr Peninsula.
- **1424 DR** — Cape Velen declares independence from Tethyr.
- **c. 1430s DR** — Cape Velen's independence proves unsustainable. Harsh economic and environmental pressures.
- **c. 1440s DR** — Velen votes to rejoin the Kingdom, conditioned on democratic representation in city and county governance.

### The Sundering & Recovery (~1480s–1492 DR)
- **1482–1487 DR** — The Second Sundering. Gods walk as mortals. Mystra returns, Weave restored.
- **1480s DR** — A new Caliphate rises in Calimshan, driving out the genies. Southern pressure on Tethyr moderates.
- **c. 1489–1490 DR** — Queen Chandaara crowned after the deaths of her parents and three older siblings.
- **1492 DR** — Present day. Shieldmeet year. The kingdom holds, but anxiously.
