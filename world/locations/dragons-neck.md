# The Dragon's Neck

## Overview
The Dragon's Neck is the eastern half of the Tethyr Peninsula, stretching westward into the Sea of Swords. Together with the Dragon's Head Peninsula (west), it forms a landmass shaped like a dragon with its maw open. The two halves are divided by **Khalid's Wall**, an ancient ruined fortification built perpendicular to the shoreline by Pasha Khalid al Axash (circa -378 DR) to contain barbarian tribes.

Almost entirely covered by the **Wealdath** (Forest of Tethir), the Dragon's Neck is wild land — geographically claimed by the Kingdom of Tethyr but only nominally controlled by human authority.

## Key Features
- Dense old-growth forest (the Wealdath) covering nearly the entire peninsula
- **Khalid's Wall** — ruined fortification dividing Dragon's Neck from Dragon's Head
- **Firedrake Road** — the main (and dangerous) overland route along the southern coast
- Coastline on both the north and south, with the Sea of Swords on all sides

## Points of Interest
- **Jhaansciim** — hamlet along the Firedrake Road, first notable settlement west of Khalid's Wall
- **Mosstone** — walled caravan-stop town on the Trade Way at the forest's edge (pop. up to 6,000 in caravan season)
- See also: [The Wealdath](the-wealdath.md), [Cape Velen](cape-velen.md)

## History
- **c. -378 DR** — Khalid's Wall built to contain barbarian tribes
- **1347 DR** — Fall of Castle Tethyr, beginning of the Tethyrian Interregnum
- **1369 DR** — Tethyr reunified under Queen Zaranda Star and King Haedrak III
- **1385 DR** — The Spellplague destabilizes the region
- **1424 DR** — Cape Velen declares independence from Tethyr due to isolation caused by Muranndin
