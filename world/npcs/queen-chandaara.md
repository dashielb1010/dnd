# Queen Chandaara Rhindaun

## Description
A young woman in her early twenties, recently crowned Queen-Monarch of Tethyr. The last surviving heir of the Rhindaun dynasty.

## Background
- Great-granddaughter (or great-great) of Queen Zaranda Star and King Haedrak III
- Fourth in the line of succession — never expected to rule
- Her father (the previous King) and mother presided over the difficult years after the Second Sundering; both have passed
- Her three older siblings — first, second, and third in line — all died tragically in **separate incidents**
- Crowned within the past 3 years (c. 1489–1490 DR)

## Motivations
- Prove herself as a capable ruler despite her youth and the kingdom's doubts
- Stabilize a nervous kingdom shaken by the deaths in the royal bloodline
- Consolidate her authority against nobles and factions who may see opportunity in a young, untested queen

## Secrets
- The separate deaths of three heirs feels like more than coincidence — is someone pruning the bloodline?

## Connections
- **Kingdom of Tethyr** — her charge, anxious under her rule
- **Cape Velen / Velen** — the semi-autonomous city-state with democratic leanings; a political complication
- **The Wealdath elves** — autonomous power within her kingdom's borders
- **The new Caliphate to the south** — a rising power that has eased pressure on Tethyr's southern border

- The kingdom's anxiety is a campaign-level tension: a young queen, a thinned bloodline, powerful neighbors
